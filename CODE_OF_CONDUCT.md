# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous about the people who raise
  them.
- Assume competence. Ask what someone tried before explaining what they should
  have tried.
- Give and accept specific, actionable review comments.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, even when you disagree; argue the
  technical case once, then move on.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, including attacks based on the characteristics
  listed above.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting code that weakens the approval, authorization, audit,
  or cost controls of this project.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments) and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Reports are
handled privately. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently remove a participant, and will explain the decision to the reporter.

# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous about the people who raise
  them.
- Assume competence. Ask what someone tried before explaining what they should
  have tried.
- Give and accept specific, actionable review comments.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, even when you disagree; argue the
  technical case once, then move on.
- Credit other people's work.
